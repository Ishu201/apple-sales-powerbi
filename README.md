# Apple Sales Performance - Power BI Dashboard
### ALY6070 Communicating & Visualizing Data Analysis | Northeastern University

## Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes Apple's FY 2024 sales performance
with a focus on the Apple Store as a direct retail channel.
The Apple Store contributes 1 in every 6 dollars of Apple's 
total 2024 revenue with no resellers or third parties involved. 
Pure direct revenue where Apple owns the customer, 
the experience, and the margin end to end.

## Business Questions
- How does the Apple Store perform compared to other sales channels?
- Which customer segments drive the highest revenue per transaction?
- What is the return risk by product category?
- How does revenue trend month-over-month across 2024?
- Does payment method influence discount behavior?

## Key Findings
- Apple Store generated **$1.0M revenue** and **$352.2K profit** 
  in FY 2024 a ~35% profit margin with +7.5% growth
- **Return rate of 8.3%** slightly above the company-wide 8.1%
- **Government buyers** recorded the highest average transaction 
  value at $1,757 above even Business customers
- **October** is the peak revenue month (~$135K), 
  driven by iPhone launch cycles
- **Net Banking** attracts the highest discount rate at ~4.6% 
  vs Debit Card at ~3.3%
- **iPhone** accounts for the largest share of product returns 
  by volume, reflecting sales dominance rather than quality issues

## Dashboard Features
- Year filter (2022 / 2023 / 2024)
- Sales Channel slicer (Apple Store, Online, Reseller, etc.)
- Item Category slicer
- KPI cards with sparklines (Revenue, Profit, Return Rate, Marketing ROI)
- Revenue Distribution by Customer Segment (bar chart)
- Monthly Revenue Performance (line chart)
- Product Return by Category (treemap)

## Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX (calculated measures, date table)
- Kaggle Apple Global Product Sales Dataset

## Dataset
- 11,500 transactions | 47 countries | 6 product categories
- Period: 2022–2024
- Source: [Kaggle Apple Global Product Sales]([https://www.kaggle.com/](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ashyou09/apple-global-product-sales-dataset))
